# Waitin' On You

People are worried about me\
They say I'm getting old\
People are calling me\
They wanna know if I still got it\
If I still got a hold\
\
But I'm just strumming along\
Trying to write the very best song, for you\
And I'm dreaming all night long\
About all them things that we could do\
\
People are worried about the world\
They say it's going down in flames\
People are worried about it all\
They can't understand\
\
But I'm just strumming along ...\
\
But if I'm completely honest\
Sure, I have my doubts\
In a week I'll be trekking in the forest\
Not sure if I'll ever be coming out\
\
People worry about me\
They say I'm too old to walk from sea to sea\
People are worried about themselves, I guess\
I'm just a puppet, something in front of their face\
\
But I'm just strumming a song\
Trying to write the very best one, for you\
I'm sitting here, with nothing to do\
Full of dreams, waitin' on you\
\
I'm full of dreams and I'm a-waiting on you
